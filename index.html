The problem

Rental enquiries don't wait for office hours.

Most come in after 6pm, on weekends, or while your team is mid-viewing with someone else. By the time anyone replies, the good ones have already messaged three other agencies.

🌙

Leads arrive off-hours

Tenants scroll listings at night. A reply the next morning is often a reply too late.

🧾

Unqualified enquiries eat your day

Budget, move-in date, pets, occupants — the same five questions, asked over and over, before you even know if it's worth a viewing.

🔁

Good leads go cold in the gap

Between "still available?" and an agent actually replying, tenants move on. Speed decides who gets the viewing.

How it works

Three steps. No new app for tenants or agents.

Penny works on your existing WhatsApp Business number — tenants message the way they already do.

01

Tenant messages your number

From a listing, a signboard, or a portal link — any hour, any day. Penny replies in seconds.

02

Penny qualifies & matches

Budget, move-in timing, pets, employment, occupants. She scores the lead and surfaces the right listings from your portfolio.

03

Viewing booked, agent notified

Penny offers slots, confirms the booking, and hands qualified leads to your team with full context — no re-asking.

The signature feature

Built for the question letting agents actually ask.

A bond calculator tells you what a buyer can borrow. It says nothing about whether a renter is a good fit for your unit. The Khaya Score weighs five things agents actually check before offering a viewing — budget fit, move-in timing, pet policy, employment status, and occupants against bedrooms — into one number your team can trust at a glance.

Why it matters: every score, and the conversation behind it, is stored against the lead — so your team never re-asks what Penny already confirmed.
